Event Rules

Code Relay

  1. Each team consists of 2 members.
  2. Round 1 has 2 minutes of discussion followed by 5 minutes for each member to code.
  3. Round 2 has 10 minutes of discussion followed by 10 minutes for each member to code.
  4. Members may communicate only during the discussion period.
  5. Member 2 must continue from the code left by Member 1.
  6. Internet, mobile phones, AI tools and external assistance are not allowed.
  7. Teams are evaluated according to correctness, test cases and timely completion.
  8. Malpractice may result in disqualification.
